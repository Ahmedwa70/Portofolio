PROPRIETARY — NOT OPEN SOURCE
================================================================================

This repository contains the original design, source code, architecture,
written content and visual identity of the personal academic portfolio of
Ahmed Sobhy ("the Work").

The Work includes, without limitation:

  * The multilingual content-management architecture separating CV_DATA
    (js/data.js), UI_TRANSLATIONS (js/translations.js) and the rendering
    engine (js/app.js), including the getLocalizedValue() resolution model
    and the nine-language translation-completeness validator.
  * The visual identity: the Deep Navy / Gold / Turquoise design tokens, the
    arched portrait treatment, and the per-script typography system with its
    optical size multipliers and bidirectional isolation rules (css/style.css).
  * All written content in all nine languages: the professional titles,
    biography, experience descriptions, teaching methodology, and every
    translated string.
  * The photographic portrait of Ahmed Sobhy (assets/images/profile.jpg).
